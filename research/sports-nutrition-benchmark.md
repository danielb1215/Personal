## Quick Read — Where Each Product Wins

| Product | Best for | Standout trait |
|---|---|---|
| **Skratch Labs** | Clean, real-fruit hydration for long sessions | Sweat-matched electrolytes, no artificial ingredients |
| **SaltStick FastChews** | On-the-move salt without a bottle | Chew-and-go, works in ~45 sec, lowest cost/serving |
| **GU Roctane** | Fuel + electrolytes in one during ultra efforts | High carbs (250 cal) + BCAAs/caffeine |
| **Terra (Titanium)** | Higher-sodium isotonic for heavy sweaters | Highest sodium per serving (~505–714 mg) |
| **Nuun Sport** | Low-sugar, portable, certified-clean hydration | Only one that is Informed Sport certified; widest retail reach |

### Positioning at a glance

- **Highest sodium/serving:** Terra (~505–714 mg) → Skratch (~400 mg) → GU (320 mg) → Nuun (300 mg) → SaltStick (100 mg, but designed to be taken multiple chews).
- **Highest carbs/calories (fueling):** GU Roctane (59–60 g / 250 cal) is a *fuel + hydration* product; the others are hydration-first (Skratch 20 g; Terra moderate–high; SaltStick & Nuun ~2 g / near-zero).
- **Lowest cost/serving:** SaltStick (~$0.50) and Nuun (~$0.67–0.75) lead; GU is the most expensive (fuel product).
- **Cleanest certification story:** Nuun (Informed Sport) is the only one with a sport-anti-doping certification in this set.
