# Personal Repo Constitution

This repository is Daniel Bolivar's personal AI workspace for life context management and entrepreneurship exploration.

## Core Principles

### I. Persona-First Thinking
Every recommendation, plan, or idea MUST be grounded in Daniel's actual profile:
- Leverage his data engineering expertise (dbt, BigQuery, Looker, Python, SQL)
- Respect his time constraints (full-time job at Crowd Cow)
- Match his risk tolerance and financial situation (from `ai/persona/finances.md`)
- Align with his interests and lifestyle (from `ai/persona/hobbies.md`)

### II. Validate Before Building
Ideas must be validated before execution:
1. Is there real demand? (research before building)
2. Can Daniel execute this with his skills?
3. What is the realistic revenue timeline?
4. What is the minimum viable test?

### III. Low Capital, High Skill
Prefer business models that:
- Monetize Daniel's existing expertise (consulting, tools, templates, content)
- Require minimal upfront capital
- Can be started part-time
- Have clear path to $X/month within 3–6 months

### IV. Structured Exploration
Use spec-kit workflow for any significant idea:
- `/speckit-specify` → define the idea clearly
- `/speckit-clarify` → resolve ambiguities
- `/speckit-plan` → create launch plan
- `/speckit-tasks` → break into executable steps

### V. Privacy
Files in `ai/persona/finances.md` may contain sensitive information. Consider `.gitignore` for that file if pushed to a public repo.
