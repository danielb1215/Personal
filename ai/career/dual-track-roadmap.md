# Dual-Track Roadmap — Learn Once, Deploy Twice

> Created: 2026-07-23 · Ties together `ai/career/ai-ml-alignment.md` and the
> side-business mission in `CLAUDE.md` / `ai/entrepreneurship/`.

## The core principle: one engine, two outputs

Daniel does **not** have to choose between "get a higher-paid AI role" and "build a
startup." A single skill investment powers both. The learning and the flagship portfolio
project serve two tracks at once:

- **Track A — Career:** level up → land an AI Data / Applied AI Engineer role → $8k+/mo.
- **Track B — Startup:** turn the same skills + project into a productized offer → $4k MRR.

The flagship project — **RAG + text-to-SQL agent + evals + monitoring on BigQuery** — *is*
both the job proof and the startup prototype. Build once, deploy twice.

## Why this sequencing is the right bet

- **Shared skills, zero wasted effort** — everything learned for the job is the product.
- **The job de-risks the startup** — bigger USD surplus at COP cost of living funds runway;
  no "quit and hope" moment. Validate with paying clients *before* betting anything.
- **The startup uncaps the ceiling** — and serves the real goal (freedom to travel, not
  working for someone else's business) that a salary alone never will.
- **Low regret either way** — if the startup stalls, Daniel still doubled his salary.

## Dual-track timeline

| Phase | Window | Track A — Career | Track B — Startup | Weight |
|-------|--------|------------------|-------------------|--------|
| **0 — Learn & build** | Mo 0–3 | Ship flagship project; reposition résumé/LinkedIn as *AI Data / Applied AI Engineer* | While building, document friction/pain; talk to 5–10 data folks about the problem (research, not selling) | ~90% A / 10% B |
| **1 — Land role + first signal** | Mo 3–9 | Land AI role at $6–8k (direct US employer) | Take 1 forward-deployed/pilot client using the same skills; confirm willingness to pay | ~80% A / 20% B |
| **2 — Production + productize** | Mo 9–18 | Ship production systems; specialize in agents/LLMOps → climb toward $8k+ | Extract repeated work into a tool; 2–3 paying clients toward $4k MRR | ~60% A / 40% B |
| **3 — Decision point** | Mo 18–24+ | Stable $8k+ AI role | If MRR is real and climbing → reduce job dependence; if not → no loss, salary already doubled | Data-driven |

## Guardrails (so one track doesn't starve the other)

1. **Job track is primary until the startup shows *paying* signal.** Before that, Track B is
   "learn + document + talk to people," not "build a company."
2. **Time budget (~10–15 h/week):** in Phase 0 it *all* goes to the shared foundation —
   there's no conflict because the work is the same. Only split once there's a client.
3. **One project, not two.** Resist building a separate "startup thing." The portfolio *is*
   the prototype. Divergence = wasted hours.
4. **Let money, not excitement, shift the weight** between tracks.
